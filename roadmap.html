FUTURE CONCEPTS

Wallet & Portfolio

Planned — Not Available Yet

With permission, view connected addresses, balances, holdings, transaction history and portfolio performance. BNB Smart Chain is the first planned network, with Solana, Base and Ethereum to follow.

Wallet authentication will use a non-custodial proof of control. Your wallet connection grants no trading permission, and this website will never ask for a seed phrase or private key.

Automated Trading

Planned — Not Available Yet

The independent Personal BSC Auto-Trader is intended to evolve into a multichain execution engine. Its strategy and risk controls will work across chains through network-specific adapters.

Future controls include allocation, concurrent positions, portfolio exposure, gas reserves, trailing stops and an emergency stop. Execution will require separate, limited, revocable permission and provide previews, status and audit logs.

Research alerts provide evidence for review. Automated trading and transaction facilitation require separate authorization and review. The Personal BSC Auto-Trader is a separate project.

Master roadmap and milestone evidence · Community

Eight phases, evidence before activation

  1. Core intelligence · in development

    Verified scanner, holders, wallet relationships, liquidity, risk and expiring passport evidence.

  2. Integrated research website · review implementation

    Usable evidence interfaces with authenticated data, source inspection and mobile accessibility.

  3. Commercial intelligence · planned

    Intelligence Pro, enterprise Risk Firewall API and Continuous Verification. Non-token payment options remain available in the design.

  4. Execution Guard and Automation Studio · planned

    Research workflows, deduplicated alerts and separately gated execution protections.

  5. Public Multichain Trading Engine · future

    Solana, BNB Chain, Ethereum and Base each require independent acceptance. Allocation, exposure, trailing stops, wallet isolation and emergency stop remain design requirements, not available services.

  6. Optional Token Utility Layer · feasibility only

    AI Meme Radar is token-independent. Issuance, tokenomics and contracts need separate owner, security and applicable legal review; no token or financial benefit is promised.

  7. Strategy Vaults and advanced services · future

    Financial services and integrity bonds require separate legal, security and owner approval.

  8. Living expansion · future

    Expand scope only when provenance, capacity, reliability and customer value are demonstrated.

No public execution, fundraising offer or investment return is authorized. The Personal BSC Auto-Trader remains separate; private wallets and credentials are never reused.
