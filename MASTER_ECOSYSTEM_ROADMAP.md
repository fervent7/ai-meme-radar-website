## 2. Programmable Intelligence and Developer Services

- **In testing.** An authenticated rule preview evaluates cached exact-pool
  evidence. The owner-scoped rule store has local version, quota and
  deduplication tests. Hosted evaluation against restored history remains open.
- **Next.** Add trusted per-user identity, durable rule storage and evaluation
  history before scheduling. Conditional Research-Triggered Agents can evaluate
  user-defined research conditions and produce explanations, watchlist events or
  research alerts. Require source evidence, freshness, auditability, deduplication
  and authorized recipients before delivery.
- **Planned.** Add wallet concentration and transfer analytics with explicit
  confidence and provenance. Label coordinated-activity signals as hypotheses
  unless the evidence supports a stronger conclusion. Design versioned read-only
  API and feed contracts, scopes and quotas. Developer services remain gated on
  access controls, stable schemas, documentation and measured operational capacity.

## 3. Advanced Liquidity and Institutional Research

- **Existing evidence.** Token reports display provider-reported pool liquidity,
  volume and balances where available. Indicative route research is in testing.
- **Next.** Validate size-specific indicative quotes, liquidity and depth changes,
  route coverage, fees, price impact, slippage and quote age. Keep exact asset
  units and distinguish reported pool balances from executable depth. Add
  evidence-backed block-transfer and thin-liquidity research.
- **Future concepts.** Institutional research reports, OTC venue and block-trade
  intelligence, and feasibility studies for vetted research rooms. Any RFQ
  matching, order routing, escrow, custody or transaction facilitation requires
  separate authorization and product, security and applicable legal review.

## 4. Sustainable Ecosystem Evolution

- **Existing.** The website hosts research, roadmap, documentation and community
  navigation. Community feedback can help prioritize extensions.
- **Future.** Consider professional workspaces, portfolio research, partnerships,
  membership tiers and supported developer services after verified utilities and
  demonstrated demand. Require secure entitlements, operational support and
  sustainable service economics before proposing paid services.
- **Later review.** Consider token utility only after a working platform, real
  users, a sustainable model and a separate feasibility, security and legal
  review. Token issuance, paid services and any public trading integration
  require separate authorization. The Personal BSC Auto-Trader stays in its own
  repository and deployment.

## Interfaces and sequencing

| Boundary | Minimum design contract |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence | Chain, token and pool identity, metric value and unit, provider, original observation time, verification and freshness state. Preserve the existing record shape as the source of truth. |
| Evaluation | Exact-pool evidence input, rule version, reason, evidence references and evaluation time. Cached preview precedes scheduled evaluations. |
| Research notifications | A separate delivery interface with consent, deduplication and audit history. Research agents have no execution capability. |
| Liquidity research | Trade size and units, indicative quote, fee and impact fields, provider, timestamp and explicit unavailable states. |
| Developer access | Versioned read-only contracts and scoped authorization. Paid access and external integrations remain future decisions. |

Near term, finish the authorized test deployment and authenticated restart
read-back. Use the saved Whale Pad report to verify both labels and original
timestamps on Android. Continue offline regression work while hosted access or
provider evidence is pending. Keep the approved branding and existing research
interface in place. Preserve the working public service and release only from
verified evidence.
